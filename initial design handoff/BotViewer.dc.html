the state law, on Nocturne's ramps
Your four hues are back where they belong — a dark ground. Each is re-derived at the lightness of accent-400, so no state outranks another by accident: paper filled and still open, green active, amber scheduled, red canceled, gray filled and finished — which is why a closed trade reads as a chain of gray marks with the odd red one where a sell had to be re-cut. Nocturne keeps chroma low outside the accent, so state lives in 3px marks and 13% tints, never a flood.
rules that fade
Nocturne's signature is a rule that dissolves at both ends. Applied here it does real work: the row rules stop competing with the state marks, and the bot rule fades to the right so the accent reads as a label for the bot rather than a border around its rows.
why the accent is blue
Blurple is out. The replacement was close to forced rather than a taste call: your state law already owns green, amber and red, and paper is the filled state — so the accent has to sit somewhere none of those four can be mistaken for, which leaves blue. It is set at the same OKLCH lightness Nocturne’s accent held, so every contrast figure in the system still holds, with the ramp regenerated on the same steps. Also functional, not visual: tabular-nums on Inter, without which the price columns stop aligning to the digit.

what this page cannot say
Three absences, all of them in the schema rather than the design. There is no commission or fee column anywhere, so every number is gross and the small-average-win bots are the ones that reading flatters most. There is no balance history — the curve is cumulative realized P&L, not what the account was worth, and it steps only when a sell fills. And bars.db carries no index, only the symbols the bots asked for, so “versus the market” here means holding the same names, not XU100.
unrealized stays out
Open positions are worth whatever the feed last said, and the feed goes quiet. Letting a stale mark into a win rate would make the number move on its own, so this page counts a trade only once its sell has filled — which is also why the Book shows realized and unrealized apart and never adds them into one score.
one chart, on purpose
Nocturne draws state in 3px marks and 13% tints, not in fills, so the accent gets the single line worth plotting and every other measure here is a number with its own denominator next to it. A page of six charts would read as six shapes; this one reads as eight sentences and a line.
